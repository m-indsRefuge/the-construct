# Environment & Contracts

World Zero now has computational consequences.

## Contracts

A contract contains input/output examples and a compute reward.

Inhabitants are tested by behavior, not by source-code appearance.

## Construction pressure

Each ecology step:

1. advances the world clock
2. chooses an inhabitant
3. exposes it to a contract
4. rewards successful behavior with compute
5. on failure, attempts mutation
6. periodically composes two inhabitants
7. records the result in the event stream

No language model is involved.

The inhabitants still have no cognition or natural-language objective. Construction
is a consequence of local rules and environmental pressure.

The next subsystem is the Observatory.
